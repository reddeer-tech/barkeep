BarKeep 0.0.2 — First public release

BarKeep is a macOS menu-bar manager and notch Dynamic Island in one app.

Menu bar manager

  • Hide & show menu-bar items in three sections: Shown, Hidden, and Always Hidden — toggle from the menu-bar chevron, hotkeys, or hover/click/scroll gestures.
  • Drag-to-arrange layout editor with live item previews: reorder within a section or move items between sections, with groups and a searchable palette.
  • Custom menu-bar appearance: tint, gradients, borders, shadows and shapes with a live preview.
  • Presets, triggers and per-action hotkeys, plus a Bartender-style reveal bar of hidden items below the menu bar.

Notch Dynamic Island

  • Live activities in the closed pill: now playing with artwork and transport, timers, upcoming meetings with Join/Copy-link buttons beside the pill, browser & App Store downloads, Focus, screen recording, camera/microphone privacy with app attribution, and local AI-agent sessions.
  • Concurrent activities: the highest-priority state owns the pill while everything else stays visible as tiny status dots.
  • A tabbed expanded shell — Home, Media, Stats, Battery, Clipboard, Weather, Color, Timer, Calendar, Notes, Reminders, Shelf, Webcam, Audio and an embedded Terminal — with configurable tab order/visibility and ⌘1–⌘9 shortcuts.
  • Calendar quick-add in natural language ("meeting with john at 7pm"), inline reminder completion, hourly + 7-day weather, clipboard history with files and images, and a drag-and-drop file shelf.
  • Works on notched and notch-less displays, multi-monitor aware, hover or click to open.

System

  • Sparkle automatic updates; runs as a quiet menu-bar agent (optional Dock icon).
  • macOS 13+, Apple Silicon and Intel (universal).
