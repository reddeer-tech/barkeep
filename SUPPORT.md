# Support

For help with BarKeep for Mac or the BarKeep phone app, including your account, Premium or a purchase,
please email admin@reddeer.ai

You can also report a bug or ask a question in GitHub Issues:
https://github.com/reddeer-tech/barkeep/issues

To delete your account, open the phone app and go to Settings, then Manage account, or follow the
steps in the Privacy Policy:
https://github.com/reddeer-tech/barkeep/blob/main/PRIVACY.md#deleting-your-account
