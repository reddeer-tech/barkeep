What's New

BarKeep 0.0.21

Friends, chat & smarter invites 👥

  • Friends. A new Friends tab in the island: add friends by their friend code or email, accept requests right from a popup under the notch, and organize friends with your own groups.
  • Chat. Message your friends 1:1 — new messages pop up as a small preview under the notch, with unread badges on the tab. Prefer not to keep history? Turn it off in Settings → Account and messages vanish after delivery.
  • Online status. Optionally share a green "online" dot with your friends (off by default — turn it on in Settings → Account).
  • Invite friends to games. Pick a friend in a game's Online lobby (or straight from the Friends tab) and BarKeep hosts the room and sends the invite in one step. Invites now show which game you're being invited to.
  • Nearby games without the dance. Your Mac can now be discovered for nearby games even when no lobby is open — the other person just opens a game and invites you (toggle in the Nearby lobby).
  • Clearer game lobby. The Online lobby no longer shows three different codes at once — one room code while hosting, a friends list front and center, and the join field only when it makes sense.

BarKeep 0.0.20

Play online, sign in, and sync your settings ☁️

  • BarKeep accounts. Create an account (Settings → Account, or right from a game lobby) and BarKeep remembers you — you stay signed in across launches.
  • Online multiplayer! The two-player games can now be played over the internet, not just your Wi-Fi: host a room, share its 6-character code (or invite a friend by their friend code), and play. The "Nearby" LAN mode is still there too.
  • Settings sync across your Macs. Your preferences — appearance, hotkeys, menu-bar layout and hide/show rules, notch behavior, even game high-scores — back up to the cloud and follow you to any Mac you sign into. Signing into a second Mac asks once whether to use your cloud settings or keep that Mac's. (Clipboard history and caches never leave your machine.)
  • New welcome step and an Account section in Settings to manage all of it.

BarKeep 0.0.19

Multiplayer games

  • Play head-to-head with a nearby Mac. Games can now connect over your local network — send an invite from the notch and play together, with a growing set of two-player games (Naval Strike, Harpoon, Sky Strike, and more).
  • Even more single-player games in the arcade.

More games & polish

BarKeep 0.0.16

Auto-updates fixed on macOS Sequoia & Tahoe

  • Automatic updates now install reliably. Some Macs (especially on the latest macOS) were showing "An error occurred while running the updater." The cause was BarKeep's internal app identifier; it's now a standard one, so macOS no longer blocks the updater. Updates from this version onward install automatically again.

One-time step when moving to this version: because the app identifier changed, macOS treats this as a fresh app — so you may need to re-grant BarKeep's permissions once (Accessibility, Screen Recording, etc.) in System Settings, and this specific update must be installed manually (drag the new BarKeep into Applications). Your settings and preferences are kept. After this one-time step, every future update is automatic.

BarKeep 0.0.15

Games in the notch

  • A whole arcade in your island. A new Games tab brings a gallery of quick mini-games — 2048, snake, Breakout, Memory Match, Lights Out, Simon, Match-3, a maze, and more — playable right in the notch or expanded to fullscreen.

Fixes

  • Fixed the notch jumping to the Shelf tab when you click-drag inside it. The "drag a file onto the notch to open the Shelf" shortcut was misfiring on ordinary click-drags (it was reacting to a leftover drag from earlier); it now triggers only for a real file drag. Dragging an actual file in still opens the Shelf as before.

BarKeep 0.0.14

Your own volume & brightness HUD — no more double overlay

  • The macOS volume/brightness overlay no longer appears when BarKeep is showing its own. BarKeep now handles the media keys itself and applies the change, so the system's overlay is never drawn — just BarKeep's clean in-notch HUD. (Fine-adjust with Shift+Option still works, and on outputs/displays BarKeep can't drive it quietly steps aside and lets macOS handle it.)
  • Fixed the volume/brightness HUD overflowing the island on notch-less displays — the pill now sizes exactly to its contents.

Even more idle animations

  • A big batch of new idle faces for the island — a bouncing DVD logo, campfire, fireworks, an aquarium, a spinning vinyl record, an hourglass, a radar sweep, Newton's cradle, a jellyfish, rain, snow, fog, falling leaves, drifting stars, and more — pick your favorite in the redesigned animation picker.

Smoother updates

  • Updated the auto-update engine to the latest version for better reliability and security.
  • If macOS ever blocks an update with its "App Management" permission prompt, BarKeep now explains what happened and opens the exact Settings pane so it's a single one-time toggle — instead of a generic "try again later" error.

Polish

  • News corner stack: "Clear all." When you have more than one headline card stacked in the corner, a Clear all control now dismisses them in one tap (the per-card ✕ still clears just one).

BarKeep 0.0.13

Private by default

  • BarKeep is invisible in screenshots and screen recordings. Every window — the island, overlays, cards, even the menu-bar icon — is automatically excluded from ⇧⌘3/⇧⌘4/⇧⌘5 captures and recordings; whatever is behind shows through as if BarKeep weren't there.

Menu bar layout, polished

  • Drops land exactly where you point. Dragging an icon into another row now places it precisely at the insertion line — including drops at the end of a row.
  • Instant feedback, self-healing drags. The layout updates the moment you drop; if macOS silently rejects a synthesized move (it happens), BarKeep verifies and retries automatically.
  • Rows keep your scroll position through refreshes, and the header actions are now clearly explained: Update List (re-read the menu bar; nothing moves) vs Sort My Bar (put drifted icons back where you filed them).

New idle animations

  • A whole collection of new idle faces for the island — firefly, fish, ghost, candle, heart, metronome, and more — with a redesigned picker.

Better first run

  • The notch island is on out of the box (floating pill on notch-less displays), the Dock icon stays hidden (it's a menu-bar app), and BarKeep starts at login automatically.

Fixes & maintenance

  • Fixed the volume/brightness overlay overflowing the island on external displays.
  • Fixed a long-standing internal error during startup (a window-geometry feedback loop present since early releases).
  • The download activity's icon is properly sized, with a new reveal-in-Finder button beside the pill while downloading.
  • About now shows all permission statuses (Accessibility, Screen Recording, Calendar, Reminders) and adds Reset system permissions (TCC) — the escape hatch when macOS shows a permission as granted but the app doesn't receive it.

BarKeep 0.0.12

In-meeting controls

  • Mute, camera, share, and leave — right at the notch. A compact control pill appears automatically beside the notch whenever you're in a call, with icons that reflect the live meeting state (mute in the app → BarKeep updates within a second). Works with Google Meet (Chrome, Safari, Edge, and more), the Zoom desktop app, and the Microsoft Teams desktop app — with no window focus stolen.
  • Browsers that don't expose a reliable control path show a read-only pill (live state, lock badge). Zoom tip: enable "Always show meeting controls" in Zoom's General settings so its controls stay reachable.
  • Off by default — enable it in Settings → Notch → Meeting (requires Accessibility).

Notification mirroring

  • Notifications, in the notch. Intercept other apps' notification banners into an interactive notch card — glance, click to open, hover to hold it. Per-app opt-in with search, an Enable all switch, and a per-app choice of notch card (hides the native banner) or native (keeps macOS's banner and its reply buttons) — either way everything lands in the new Notifications history tab.
  • Off by default — enable it in Settings → Notch → Notifications (requires Accessibility).

News

  • Follow more than searches — curated topic sections (World, Business, Technology, Sports, …), local headlines for any place, and any RSS/Atom feed URL (BBC, Hacker News, a subreddit…) with real article images where feeds provide them.
  • Corner news cards no longer disturb your typing — the stack window is passive and never takes focus (fixed a caret-stealing bug), and it pauses its dwell timers while you hover.

Polish

  • The notch stays its native size unless content truly needs more room — activities no longer widen the pill for no reason on external displays (hardware-notch screens keep their around-the-cutout layout).
  • Redesigned side-action pills — timer, meeting, and the new download actions (reveal in Finder / dismiss) share one compact dark pill with crisp SF-symbol icons, stay visible while you work in other apps, and never flash tooltips over themselves.
  • Mic-in-use attribution shows the full app name on external displays (no more "zoom.…"), and stays glyph-only next to the hardware notch.
  • New option to hide the Dock icon (Settings → General).
