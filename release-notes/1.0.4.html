What's New

BarKeep 1.0.4

Calendar and notes 📝

  • Notes open in their own screen in the island, with room to read and edit.
  • The event editor grew up — set alerts, repeat rules, and which calendar or list an item belongs to, with a proper date and time picker.
  • Times follow your Mac's 12- and 24-hour setting wherever BarKeep shows them.

BarKeep 1.0.3

Your icons stay clear of the island 🏝

  • Menu-bar icons no longer creep under the notch. BarKeep reserves exactly the space the island needs and holds it steady, instead of nudging your icons around as the island changes width.
  • It works from a cold start, not just after the island has been opened once, and it repairs itself if something else shifts your bar.

Calendar and reminders, in the island 📅

  • Tap an event or reminder to open it right there — full details without leaving BarKeep, and an editor for changing them.

Fixes 🐛

  • Esc and ⌘W now close what you'd expect, working back out through search, then any open detail, then the island itself.
  • ⌘F opens the search box for the tab you're actually on.

BarKeep 1.0.2

Calendar and reminders 📅

  • A red "now" line in the day list, the way Calendar draws it, and the list centres itself on the current time when you open it.
  • New events go to the calendar you choose — pick your default in Settings → Notch Island → Calendar.
  • Tap a reminder in the timeline to jump straight to it in the Reminders tab.
  • Fixed: quick-add read some dates as the wrong century. Typing a short date like 8/5/26 could be stored as 1926.
  • Quick-add is ready to type the moment it opens — the title field takes focus immediately, including when you open it with the keyboard shortcut.

Fixes 🐛

  • Fixed: the menu bar flashing over the island when you clicked a tab. Focusing a tab briefly dropped the island beneath the menu bar, and it took up to two seconds to right itself. It no longer slips at all.
  • Fixed: permissions being attributed to the wrong copy of BarKeep, which could make macOS forget grants you had already given.

BarKeep 1.0.1

Meetings don't slow BarKeep down any more 🎥

  • Fixed: clicks getting swallowed while you were on a call. BarKeep's volume and brightness keys

sit in a checkpoint that every input event passes through, and that checkpoint was waiting behind BarKeep's own interface. During a call it could hold your input for several seconds — which is why clicks needed a second try, windows stopped responding, and text selected itself as you moved the mouse.

  • Fixed: the island crawling open during meetings. Reading the meeting's mute and camera state

meant walking the meeting window's entire accessibility tree — thousands of lookups — on the same thread that draws everything. It now happens in the background, so the island, News and every tab stay responsive mid-call.

  • The island now goes still during calls. While your microphone or camera is live, the music

spectrum, the scrolling title and the idle animation pause. They pick straight back up when the call ends. Video calls already push your Mac's display system hard, and BarKeep no longer adds to it for animations you aren't looking at.

Menu-bar arranging behaves itself 🖱

  • Fixed: your pointer appearing to move on its own after BarKeep rearranged an icon. Your mouse

is now released for the moment the move takes, so the cursor ends up where you actually put it.

  • Fixed: the reveal bar appearing while you dragged items between the shown and hidden sections

— BarKeep was mistaking its own arranging for one of your gestures.

New: AI Sessions 🤖

  • A new AI Sessions page in Settings, with a matching tab in the mobile companion app.

BarKeep 1.0.0

The first stable release. BarKeep requires macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.

One app for your menu bar and your notch ✨

  • Tidy the menu bar — a Hidden section and an Always-Hidden section, revealed by click, hover, a two-finger swipe, a hot corner, a keyboard shortcut, or a drop-down bar. Drag your icons into the order you want and it sticks.
  • Presets and triggers so your bar changes itself depending on the app you're in, the display you're on, or the time of day.
  • Nothing gets lost. When macOS runs out of room it silently drops icons; BarKeep moves them somewhere you can still reach them.
  • Style it — tint, opacity, gradients, borders, rounded corners, or split the bar around the notch.

The notch, put to work 🏝

An island over the notch — or a floating pill if your Mac hasn't got one — with Home blocks you arrange yourself, plus Music, Calendar, Meetings, Weather, News, Email, Notifications, Files & Shelf, Clipboard, Notes, AI Agents, Battery, Live Activities and a built-in Terminal. Volume and brightness get proper HUDs instead of the system ones.

Friends, chat and games 👥

Create an account to sync your settings between Macs, add friends, chat, see who's around, and play together — over the local network or online.

BarKeep Premium 💳

Everything on your own Mac and your local network stays free, forever. That includes accounts, friends, presence, reading messages, and playing over LAN.

Premium covers the three things that cost us server time: hosting online games (your friends can always join for free), sending messages (receiving is always free), and pushing your settings to the cloud (pulling them back is free). Available monthly or as a one-time lifetime purchase — and if you're on monthly you can move to lifetime any time, with what you've already paid this period credited.

Also in this release

  • A Feedback page in Settings, so you can tell us what's wrong without leaving the app.
  • Settings now looks properly native — a floating sidebar, grouped rows, and it follows your Mac's sidebar size.
  • Crash reports carry readable stack traces, so problems get fixed rather than guessed at.
