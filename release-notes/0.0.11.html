What's New

BarKeep 0.0.11

News

  • Choose where news appears — a new single-choice control lets you show headlines in the menu bar (a scrolling ticker), on the notch (a brief pill peek), or as a corner notification stack.
  • Corner notification stack — beautiful headline cards stacked in the screen corner you pick (top/bottom, left/right), each with the article's image, source, and one-tap Open and Copy link. Cards linger for an adjustable dwell time.

Fixes & polish

  • The settings window no longer opens on every launch — BarKeep stays a quiet menu-bar agent. Open it anytime from the menu-bar icon; it only appears on its own right after first-run setup.

Earlier in 0.0.x

  • Download progress shows the real downloaded size (with a true percentage bar for Safari and App Store downloads).
  • Custom Macros — type a trigger like EST# and BarKeep replaces it with text, dynamic tokens ({date}, {uuid}, {clipboard}), a shell script's output, a list pick, or a keystroke sequence.
  • Reserve space behind the notch so menu-bar icons don't hide under the cutout.
  • Optional precise-location weather (skippable; IP-based otherwise).

System

  • Runs as a quiet menu-bar agent; universal (Apple Silicon + Intel); auto-updating via Sparkle.
