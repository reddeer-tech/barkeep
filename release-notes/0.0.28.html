What's New

BarKeep 0.0.28

BarKeep runs on more Macs now 💻

  • BarKeep now supports macOS 15 Sequoia and later. Through a build mistake every previous release was quietly built to require the very newest macOS — so on anything older it would download, install, and then refuse to open. If BarKeep wouldn't start on your Mac, this release is the fix.

A way out of the reveal bar ✕

  • The hidden-items bar now has a close button. Every way of dismissing it was a toggle of whatever opened it, so if you opened it from the app-icon menu without knowing the keyboard shortcut, there was no visible way to close it again.

Terminal has its own settings 🖥

  • Terminal moved out of Tabs & Widgets into its own settings page, grouped into Shell & session, Appearance, and Behavior.
  • New: a starting folder, a startup command, and a login-shell toggle — plus the shell, font and colour-theme options that were already there but had nowhere to be set. Shell settings apply on the next Restart shell.

BarKeep 0.0.27

A Home tab you can arrange 🏠

  • Home is now made of blocks you can reorder and hide — devices, media, weather, attention, activity and the calendar timeline. Drag them into your order in Settings → Notch Island → Home.
  • Blocks only take space when they have something to show. Nothing playing? The media block steps aside and the weather spreads out with the hours ahead. Everything quiet? Home stays short and calm.
  • Weather got richer: your place name, today's high/low, current conditions and the next five hours — laid out side by side when there's room.
  • All-day events now appear above the timeline (they were invisible before), and the next-event line shows the start time and room.
  • Attention chips: unread chats, new notifications and working agents at a glance. Click the bell to open Notifications — and just viewing them clears the count, no deleting required.
  • Tap the media block to jump to the Music tab. The previous/next buttons now use the correct icons, and 15-second skip buttons joined the transport.

The seek bar works now 🎯

  • Dragging the music progress bar actually changes the playback position. It silently did nothing before, for every player.

Weather in the right place 📍

  • Fixed weather showing a faraway default city whenever the location lookup failed — and it could never recover, even with Refresh. Your location now persists across restarts, Refresh re-checks your precise position, and the label shows your actual place name instead of "My Location".
  • Fixed the hourly forecast showing another timezone's hours — the current temperature and the "Now" column could disagree in the same view.

Battery knows your devices 🔋

  • The Battery tab lists your connected Bluetooth devices with their charge — including AirPods left, right and case separately.
  • Devices that only report battery through one of macOS's two channels now all show up (a keyboard and a mouse could each appear in one place but not the other).
  • The header battery capsule got a cleaner look: no stray tip, a flat fill edge, and a two-tone percentage that stays readable at any level.

Fixes 🐛

  • Album artwork on Home is now properly rounded.
  • The calendar Join button no longer stays live for a meeting that already ended when the island reopens after a long break.
  • Reopening the island after hours away no longer shows stale timeline data for up to 30 seconds.

BarKeep 0.0.26

Fixes 🐛

  • The music pill now sizes correctly. A layout calculation was failing whenever a track was playing, so the island didn't widen to fit the album art and title.
  • The process list no longer moves under your pointer. It re-sorted every couple of seconds, so a busy process would slide away as you reached for it — and a refresh landing just before your click could quit the wrong one. While your pointer is over the list the order now holds still; the numbers keep updating.
  • Crash reports now include readable stack traces, so problems can be diagnosed properly rather than guessed at.

Smaller touches ✨

  • Clipboard rows confirm a copy with a ✓, so you can tell a successful copy from a missed click.

BarKeep 0.0.25

Crash reporting 🛡

  • BarKeep now reports crashes and errors automatically, so problems get fixed without you having to describe them. Nothing you type, copy or store is included.
  • A new input watchdog. If BarKeep's menu-bar tidy-up ever leaves your mouse button or a modifier key stuck — the cause of "I have to click everything twice" — the app now notices within moments, releases it for you, and reports it so it can be fixed properly.

BarKeep 0.0.24

Typing in the island is fixed 🐛

  • Fixed: you couldn't type into the island after opening it by hover. Sign in, add a friend, quick notes, the terminal and the game code box all looked focused but ignored the keyboard. Clicking into a field now works every time. (This one slipped into 0.0.23 — sorry.)
  • Fixed: the menu-bar tidy-up could keep retrying forever, warping the pointer while it did. It now gives up instead of fighting your mouse.

Message editing, unsend, and cleaning up chats 💬

  • Edit or unsend a message you just sent — right-click it (within 15 minutes). Edited messages show a small "edited" note to both of you.
  • Delete a conversation from a friend's ⋯ menu, choosing for me (your copy only — they keep theirs) or for everyone.
  • Unfriending, blocking and deleting now all ask for confirmation first.

Away status 🌙

  • Friends now see away (amber) when your Mac is locked, the screensaver is on, or you've been idle — and plain grey when BarKeep isn't running. Set the idle delay in Settings → Account, or turn it off.
  • Messages still arrive while you're away, so nothing is missed.

Settings 🧹

  • "Reset defaults" on every Island page — reset just that page instead of everything, and it syncs to your other Macs.
  • Destructive actions (erase all settings, reset permissions) now show a proper confirmation dialog.
  • Enter signs you in from the account screens, and Tab moves between fields everywhere.
  • The permissions screen gained a "Reset system permissions" button for when macOS gets stuck showing a permission as denied.
  • Version and copyright now show at the bottom of About.

BarKeep 0.0.23

Smoother mouse, cloud sync for notes, and unread badges ✨

  • Fixed: clicks sometimes needing two attempts, and text getting "selected" on its own. While BarKeep tidied your menu bar it could briefly swallow or race your real mouse input — arranging now waits for a quiet moment, always cleans up after itself, and even repairs a stuck mouse state left behind if the app was force-quit mid-move.
  • The island no longer takes your keyboard uninvited. Opening it by hover never moves your typing focus anymore; only clicking into it does — and when it closes, focus goes straight back to the app you were using.
  • Click the island to keep it open. A click inside the open island now reliably holds it open until you click anywhere else — hovering away won't dismiss it.
  • Unread message badges everywhere. New messages now show a chat bubble with a red count on the closed island, next to the badge on the Friends tab — so you can see unread chats at a glance without opening anything.
  • Notes sync, and a new Sync settings page. Your island notes (including lists) now back up to your account and follow you across Macs. The new Settings → Sync page puts everything in one place, with per-category switches — notes, menu-bar layout, island layout, automations, and clipboard pins — all on by default.
  • Cloud settings apply instantly. Settings pulled from your account now take effect immediately on the other Mac — no relaunch needed.
  • Fixed a crash when opening the screen-recording permission page in System Settings.

BarKeep 0.0.22

Friends & chat fixes 🛠

  • Fixed: you couldn't type in the "add a friend" box. Clicking the field looked like it focused, but keystrokes went nowhere. Same fix applied to the group name fields.
  • Your online status is now on by default. Friends — and only friends — see a green dot when BarKeep is running. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Account → Friends & chat.
  • Privacy fixes for blocking. Blocking someone is now completely invisible to them: nothing in the app reveals that they've been blocked.
  • Fixed friends being stuck showing as "online" after they'd quit, and a few cases where typing a wrong friend code too many times could temporarily stop you sending requests.

BarKeep 0.0.21

Friends, chat & smarter invites 👥

  • Friends. A new Friends tab in the island: add friends by their friend code or email, accept requests right from a popup under the notch, and organize friends with your own groups.
  • Chat. Message your friends 1:1 — new messages pop up as a small preview under the notch, with unread badges on the tab. Prefer not to keep history? Turn it off in Settings → Account and messages vanish after delivery.

BarKeep 0.0.20

Play online, sign in, and sync your settings ☁️

  • BarKeep accounts. Create an account (Settings → Account, or right from a game lobby) and BarKeep remembers you — you stay signed in across launches.
  • Online multiplayer! The two-player games can now be played over the internet, not just your Wi-Fi: host a room, share its 6-character code (or invite a friend by their friend code), and play. The "Nearby" LAN mode is still there too.

Your preferences — appearance, hotkeys, menu-bar layout and hide/show rules, notch behavior, even game high-scores — back up to the cloud and follow you to any Mac you sign into. Signing into a second Mac asks once whether to use your cloud settings or keep that Mac's. (Clipboard history and caches never leave your machine.)
  • New welcome step and an Account section in Settings to manage all of it.

BarKeep 0.0.16

Auto-updates fixed on macOS Sequoia & Tahoe

  • Automatic updates now install reliably. Some Macs (especially on the latest macOS) were showing "An error occurred while running the updater." The cause was BarKeep's internal app identifier; it's now a standard one, so macOS no longer blocks the updater. Updates from this version onward install automatically again.

One-time step when moving to this version: because the app identifier changed, macOS treats this as a fresh app — so you may need to re-grant BarKeep's permissions once (Accessibility, Screen Recording, etc.) in System Settings, and this specific update must be installed manually (drag the new BarKeep into Applications). Your settings and preferences are kept. After this one-time step, every future update is automatic.

BarKeep 0.0.12

In-meeting controls

  • Mute, camera, share, and leave — right at the notch. A compact control pill appears automatically beside the notch whenever you're in a call, with icons that reflect the live meeting state (mute in the app → BarKeep updates within a second). Works with Google Meet (Chrome, Safari, Edge, and more), the Zoom desktop app, and the Microsoft Teams desktop app — with no window focus stolen.
  • Browsers that don't expose a reliable control path show a read-only pill (live state, lock badge). Zoom tip: enable "Always show meeting controls" in Zoom's General settings so its controls stay reachable.
  • Off by default — enable it in Settings → Notch → Meeting (requires Accessibility).

Notification mirroring

  • Notifications, in the notch. Intercept other apps' notification banners into an interactive notch card — glance, click to open, hover to hold it. Per-app opt-in with search, an Enable all switch, and a per-app choice of notch card (hides the native banner) or native (keeps macOS's banner and its reply buttons) — either way everything lands in the new Notifications history tab.
  • Off by default — enable it in Settings → Notch → Notifications (requires Accessibility).

News

  • Follow more than searches — curated topic sections (World, Business, Technology, Sports, …), local headlines for any place, and any RSS/Atom feed URL (BBC, Hacker News, a subreddit…) with real article images where feeds provide them.
  • Corner news cards no longer disturb your typing — the stack window is passive and never takes focus (fixed a caret-stealing bug), and it pauses its dwell timers while you hover.

Polish

  • The notch stays its native size unless content truly needs more room — activities no longer widen the pill for no reason on external displays (hardware-notch screens keep their around-the-cutout layout).
  • Redesigned side-action pills — timer, meeting, and the new download actions (reveal in Finder / dismiss) share one compact dark pill with crisp SF-symbol icons, stay visible while you work in other apps, and never flash tooltips over themselves.
  • Mic-in-use attribution shows the full app name on external displays (no more "zoom.…"), and stays glyph-only next to the hardware notch.
  • New option to hide the Dock icon (Settings → General).
