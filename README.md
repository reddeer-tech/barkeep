# 🍸 BarKeep

**A menu bar manager and notch Dynamic Island for macOS — in one app.**

Tidy your menu bar, and turn the notch into a living hub.

BarKeep keeps the top of your screen calm and useful. It hides the menu-bar clutter you don't need, brings back what you do, and turns the MacBook notch into a Dynamic Island for music, timers, meetings, the weather, and more.

## Menu bar manager

- **Hide & show items** in three sections — Shown, Hidden, and Always Hidden — from the chevron, a hotkey, or hover/click/scroll.
- **Drag-to-arrange layout editor** with live previews: reorder within a section or move items between them, with groups and a searchable palette.
- **Custom appearance** — tint, gradients, borders, shadows, and shapes, with a live preview.
- **Presets, triggers, and per-item hotkeys**, plus a floating reveal bar for hidden items.

## Notch Dynamic Island

- **Live activities in the pill** — now playing with artwork, timers, upcoming meetings, downloads, Focus, screen recording, camera/mic privacy with app attribution, and local AI-agent sessions. Concurrent activities stack as tidy status dots.
- **A tabbed expanded shell** — Home, Media, Stats, Battery, Clipboard, Weather, Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Shelf, Webcam, Audio, Terminal, and News — with configurable order and ⌘1–9 shortcuts.
- **Natural-language quick-add** for calendar and reminders, hourly + 7-day weather, clipboard history with files and images, and a drag-and-drop file shelf.

## Custom Macros

- **Text expansion** — type a trigger like `EST#` and BarKeep replaces it with your text (with `{date}`, `{uuid}`, `{clipboard}` tokens), a shell script's output, a pick from a list, or a replayed keystroke sequence. Trigger by typed abbreviation or global hotkey.

## System

- **Universal** — runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Auto-updating** — via Sparkle.
- Runs as a quiet menu-bar agent; code-signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Install

1. [Download the DMG](https://github.com/reddeer-tech/barkeep/releases/latest/download/BarKeep.dmg) (or pick a version on the [Releases](https://github.com/reddeer-tech/barkeep/releases) page).
2. Open it and drag **BarKeep** to Applications.
3. On first launch, grant **Accessibility** so BarKeep can manage your menu bar. Screen Recording is recommended for crisp item icons; other permissions are optional and asked for only when needed.

## Updates

The app checks this repository's Sparkle appcast for updates: [`appcast-macos.xml`](appcast-macos.xml).
